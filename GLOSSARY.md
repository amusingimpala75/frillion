# Frillion

Combined scoring for a Krillion player and their friends.

## Language

**Team**:
The signed-in player and all friends whose answers are available.

**Team score**:
The sum of the highest available score for each question across the team.
_Avoid_: Maximum score, combined personal scores

**Contribution**:
A player's share of the team score. A question's best score is divided equally among the players tied for that score.
